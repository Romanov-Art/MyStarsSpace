# MyStarsSpace — Zero-Budget Marketing Strategy v2

**Product:** personalized digital star map poster / printable PDF  
**Main market focus:** United States and English-speaking buyers  
**Core positioning:** a unique personalized gift based on the real night sky from a meaningful moment  
**Secondary advantage:** easy to create, instant to download, simple to print locally  
**Important correction:** “Forgot a gift? Create one in 10 minutes” is not the main brand hook. It is a strong conversion scenario and a separate content/SEO cluster.

---

## 1. Strategic Foundation

MyStarsSpace should not be positioned primarily as a “last-minute gift.” That angle is useful, but if it becomes the main message, the product starts to feel like an emergency solution instead of a meaningful premium-personalized gift.

The stronger positioning is:

> **A unique personalized gift that captures the exact sky above a meaningful moment.**

The second layer is:

> **It is simple, fast, digital, and easy to print anywhere.**

The “forgot a gift” angle should be used as a tactical acquisition hook:

> **Forgot a gift? You can still create something personal and thoughtful in minutes.**

This means the marketing system must be built around life moments first, and speed second.

---

## 2. Positioning Hierarchy

### Level 1 — Main Brand Meaning

**A one-of-a-kind gift made from the real stars above a real moment.**

This is the main emotional promise. People are not buying a PDF. They are buying a memory made visible.

### Level 2 — Personal Story

**The sky from your first date, wedding night, baby’s birth, first kiss, proposal, graduation, or the day everything changed.**

This makes the product specific and emotional.

### Level 3 — Scientific Trust

**Calculated from date, time, and location data. Not a random decorative star pattern.**

This separates the product from generic posters and low-effort printable gifts.

### Level 4 — Ease and Speed

**Create it online, download instantly, print locally, and give it today.**

This removes friction and makes the purchase feel safe.

### Level 5 — Last-Minute Rescue

**Forgot the gift? You can still give something that feels deeply personal.**

This is a strong campaign angle, but it should not define the whole brand.

## 4. Message Pyramid

All pages, videos, Pinterest pins, captions, and templates should follow this order:

1. **Moment** — what life event is this about?
2. **Emotion** — why does this moment matter?
3. **Uniqueness** — why is this gift impossible to buy ready-made?
4. **Accuracy** — why is it not just a random pretty design?
5. **Ease** — how fast and simple is it?
6. **Action** — create the map, download it, print it, give it.

Bad order:

> “Download a PDF in 10 minutes.”

Better order:

> “Give her the stars from the night you first met. Create the map in minutes and print it today.”

## 5. Brand Voice

The tone should be warm, simple, human, and confident.

### Use This Style

- “Give them the sky from the moment everything changed.”
- “A gift made from your date, your place, your story.”
- “Not another mug. Not another candle. A moment they can hang on the wall.”
- “Create it in minutes, but make it feel unforgettable.”
- “The stars above the day your family grew.”
- “A personal gift for people who already have everything.”

### Avoid This Style

- Do not lead with “cheap printable PDF.”
- Do not make the brand feel like only a panic gift.
- Do not overuse technical astronomy terms in emotional content.
- Do not sell only themes like “navy,” “black,” or “beige.”
- Do not make fake luxury claims.
- Do not use mystical promises like “the universe chose you.”

## 9. Page Structure for Each Semantic Landing Page

Every landing page should follow this structure:

### 1. Hero

- Clear life situation in the headline.
- Emotional promise.
- Product preview.
- CTA to create a map.

### 2. Why This Gift Works

Explain why this specific gift fits this exact occasion.

### 3. How It Works

Three steps:

1. Choose date, time, and location.
2. Personalize names, message, and design.
3. Download and print.

### 4. Template Gallery

Show three to six templates for that page’s occasion.

### 5. Personalization Options

Show text, colors, fonts, star layers, sizes, and print formats.

### 6. Scientific Accuracy

Explain simply:

> The map is calculated from the date, time, and location you enter.

### 7. Printing and Delivery

Explain that the product is digital and printable.

### 8. FAQ

Answer objections specific to the page.

### 9. Final CTA

Repeat the emotional promise and action.

## 14. Short Video Strategy

Platforms:

- TikTok;
- YouTube Shorts;
- Instagram Reels;
- Facebook Reels.

Recommended format:

- 9:16 vertical;
- 7–20 seconds for fast hooks;
- 20–45 seconds for story-based demos;
- no heavy explanation;
- show the result quickly;
- use captions on screen;
- show the poster in a real-life context.

### Core Short Video Formula

1. Hook with a life situation.
2. Show the emotional idea.
3. Show quick creation.
4. Show final poster/mockup.
5. CTA to create the same template.

### Example Structure

**Hook:** “Gift idea for the girl who remembers your first date.”  
**Scene:** user selects location and date.  
**Reveal:** star map poster appears.  
**Text:** “The sky from the night you met.”  
**CTA:** “Make yours in minutes.”
