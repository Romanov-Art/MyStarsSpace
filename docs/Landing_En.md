# MyStarsSpace Landing Page Design Brief — U.S. Market Version

## 1. Purpose of this document

Create a landing page design for MyStarsSpace, a digital personalized star map product for the U.S. market.

The landing page must not sell “a PDF with stars.” It must sell a meaningful, personal, beautifully designed gift: the night sky from the exact moment someone was born, got married, met their partner, moved into a new home, started a new chapter, or shared a memory worth keeping.

The page should make the visitor feel:

- this is personal, thoughtful, and easy to gift;
- it can be created in minutes;
- it looks polished and premium;
- it is based on real astronomical calculations;
- it is a digital file, so there is no shipping delay;
- it can be printed locally, framed, emailed, or used as a last-minute gift.

The core emotional promise:

**Turn a meaningful moment into a custom map of the stars above it.**

---

## 2. Product overview

MyStarsSpace is an interactive star map generator. A customer chooses the date, time, and location of a meaningful event, adds personal text, customizes the visual style, and receives a high-resolution digital poster file ready for printing.

Core product capabilities:

- generate a star map for any date, time, and location;
- city search with a large global city database;
- display stars, constellations, the Milky Way, grid lines, and cardinal directions;
- choose from multiple map themes;
- customize title, subtitle, quote, names, and typography;
- select poster size;
- export a high-resolution print-ready PDF;
- support multiple languages and currencies;
- use ready-made templates for common gift occasions.

For the U.S. landing page, the product should be positioned as a thoughtful digital gift that feels premium, personal, and practical.

---

## 3. Main landing page goal

Primary goal:

**Drive users to create and purchase a personalized digital star map.**

Secondary goals:

- explain the emotional value within the first few seconds;
- show that the product is easy to create;
- show that it is suitable for many American gift occasions;
- build trust around astronomical accuracy;
- make the digital format feel like a benefit, not a limitation;
- show realistic use cases: print at home, print locally, frame it, send it digitally;
- visually separate MyStarsSpace from generic poster stores and cold technical tools.

## 4. U.S. audience positioning

### Main audience

People looking for a thoughtful, personal, easy-to-buy gift:

- partners and spouses;
- parents of a newborn;
- newlyweds and engaged couples;
- people buying anniversary gifts;
- people shopping for Valentine’s Day;
- people looking for Mother’s Day or Father’s Day gifts;
- family members buying for birthdays, graduations, baby showers, or housewarmings;
- friends looking for something personal but not overly expensive;
- people who need a meaningful last-minute gift.

### American buying mindset

The U.S. audience usually responds well to:

- clear value and convenience;
- personalization;
- self-expression;
- family milestones;
- emotional but not overly dramatic copy;
- honest trust signals;
- straightforward “what do I get?” explanations;
- simple purchase flow;
- visual proof of quality.

Avoid overly poetic or vague copy. American gift-commerce pages usually perform better when emotion is paired with practicality.

Example of the right tone:

> A custom star map of the night your story began — ready to download, print, and gift today.

Example of the wrong tone:

> The infinite universe preserves the sacred mystery of your destiny.

That sounds too abstract, too mystical, and less trustworthy.

## 6. Key value propositions

### 6.1. A gift that feels personal

Every map is connected to a specific person, place, date, time, and message. This makes it feel made for someone, not simply bought for them.

### 6.2. Ready today

The product is digital. The customer does not wait for shipping. They can download the file, print it locally, frame it, or send it instantly.

This should be treated as a major advantage for the U.S. market, especially around Valentine’s Day, Christmas, anniversaries, birthdays, and last-minute gifting.

### 6.3. Works for many life moments

The product is not only romantic. The page must show many use cases:

- wedding;
- anniversary;
- Valentine’s Day;
- birth of a baby;
- baby shower;
- birthday;
- Mother’s Day;
- Father’s Day;
- Christmas;
- graduation;
- housewarming;
- first home;
- first date;
- engagement;
- pet adoption;
- memorial gift;
- business launch or first sale.

### 6.4. Professional design

The final result should feel like a polished interior poster, not a generated image from a basic online tool.

### 6.5. Astronomical accuracy

The star map is calculated based on the selected date, time, and location. This must be explained simply and confidently.

Do not overpromise with claims like “NASA certified” unless there is proof. Use honest wording:

- “based on astronomical calculations”;
- “calculated for your selected date, time, and location”;
- “not a random star pattern.”

### 6.6. Flexible customization

The customer can personalize the text, location, date, language, map style, colors, fonts, size, and visible sky elements.

## 9. Typography direction

### Requirements

- Headings: elegant, modern, slightly emotional, premium.
- Body text: clean, simple, highly readable.
- UI labels: clear sans-serif.
- Decorative fonts should be shown inside poster templates only, not used heavily across the landing UI.

### Recommended direction

- Headings: modern serif or soft display serif.
- Body: clean sans-serif.
- Buttons and UI: strong readable sans-serif.

Typography must work well on mobile. Do not use thin, low-contrast text for important content.

## 10.11. FAQ section

### Goal

Remove final objections before purchase.

### FAQ copy

#### Is this a real star map or just a design?

It is a star map calculated from the date, time, and location you enter. The final design is personalized, but the sky is based on your selected moment.

#### What if I do not know the exact time?

You can use an approximate time. For some gifts, evening, night, noon, or the time of the ceremony is enough. The more accurate the time, the more accurate the sky map.

#### Can I create a map for a past date?

Yes. You can create a map for a birth date, wedding day, first date, engagement, graduation, move-in day, or any other meaningful date.

#### Will I receive a physical poster?

The base product is a digital file. You download it and print it wherever you prefer.

#### Is the file ready for printing?

Yes. You receive a high-resolution file prepared for the poster size you choose.

#### Can I change the text on the map?

Yes. You can add names, location, date, time, and a personal message.

#### Can I give it as a last-minute gift?

Yes. Because it is digital, you can create, download, and gift it the same day.

#### Where can I print it?

You can print it at home, at a local print shop, or through an online printing service.

#### Can I send it digitally?

Yes. You can send the file as a digital gift, then print it later if you want.

#### Can I choose a different language?

Yes. The product supports multiple languages for the interface and map text.

#### Is it good for a baby shower?

Yes. You can use the baby’s due date if the baby has not arrived yet, or the actual birth date and time after birth.

#### Is this only for couples?

No. It works for babies, families, birthdays, graduations, pets, memorials, new homes, and business milestones too.

# 12. U.S. copy rules

### Use clear emotional language

Good:

> A custom map of the night your family began.

Weak:

> Personalized stellar visualization for family-related events.

### Pair emotion with practicality

Good:

> Download it today, print it locally, and frame it in time for the celebration.

Weak:

> A touching gift for all eternity.

### Avoid too much mysticism

This is not an astrology product. It is a personalized astronomy-based gift.

Avoid:

- destiny;
- fate;
- cosmic signs;
- universe chose you;
- your love was written by the stars.

Use instead:

- the sky above your moment;
- the night your story began;
- a real date, place, and memory;
- calculated for your chosen moment.

### Avoid fake urgency

Do not use pushy copy like:

- Only 3 left;
- Hurry before it disappears;
- Secret limited offer.

Digital products do not need fake scarcity. Use real convenience instead:

> Create it today. Gift it today.

# 22. Final design quality standard

The final landing page should feel:

- warm;
- trustworthy;
- giftable;
- personal;
- polished;
- easy;
- accurate;
- mobile-first;
- premium but not intimidating.

The design should make the digital format feel like freedom:

**No shipping. No waiting. No generic gift. Just your moment, ready to print and give.**
